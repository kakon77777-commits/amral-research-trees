# Sources

## Banwait–Huang

Barinder S. Banwait, Xiaoyu Huang,  
*On the identification of elliptic curves that admit infinitely many twists satisfying the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture*,  
arXiv:2601.16044v3.

Used for:
- known BSD verification background below conductor 5000;
- Proposition 2.9;
- Remarks 2.10–2.11;
- Theorem 2.14.

https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16044

## Fouquet–Wan

Olivier Fouquet, Xin Wan,  
*The Iwasawa Main Conjecture for universal families of modular motives*,  
arXiv:2107.13726v3.

For referee replay, prefer the simple sufficient hypotheses of Theorem 1.1 and the rank-zero p-part consequence Corollary 1.10; compare against Theorem 1.7.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13726
