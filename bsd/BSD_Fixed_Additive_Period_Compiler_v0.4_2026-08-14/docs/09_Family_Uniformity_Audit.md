# 09｜Family Uniformity Audit

The Manin constant is a global invariant of the modular parametrization.

Even when:

\[
E^{(d)}\simeq_{\mathbf Q_p}E
\]

because the quadratic character is locally trivial at a fixed bad prime \(p\),
one cannot silently infer:

\[
v_p(c_{E^{(d)}})=v_p(c_E).
\]

The conductor and modular parametrization change at the support primes of \(d\).

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{local twist triviality}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{Manin-constant invariance}.
}
\]

This is why CNS modular-degree certificates are normally per twist, while
Edixhoven's local theorem is genuinely family-uniform.
