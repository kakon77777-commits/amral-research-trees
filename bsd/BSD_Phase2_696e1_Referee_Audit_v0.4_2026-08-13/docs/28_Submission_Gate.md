## Suggested paper framing

不要一開始寫：

> We prove a fundamentally new BSD theorem.

較安全：

> We isolate and verify an explicit non-semistable quadratic-twist family obtained by
> combining recent 2-primary twist results with ordinary/multiplicative Iwasawa results
> and the arbitrary-reduction theorem of Fouquet–Wan.

若 novelty search證明 general theorem已涵蓋此例，文章仍可轉成：

- explicit corollary；
- theorem-applicability note；
- proof-engineering / algorithmic extension；
- non-semistable extension of Banwait–Huang's certificate router。
