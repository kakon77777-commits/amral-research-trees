# Sources

## Banwait–Huang 2026

Barinder S. Banwait, Xiaoyu Huang,
*On the identification of elliptic curves that admit infinitely many twists satisfying the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture*,
arXiv:2601.16044v3.

Key sections:

- Remark 2.10: semistability is a strong restriction and discusses non-semistable odd-$p$ extensions.
- Remark 2.11: explicitly identifies Fouquet–Wan as a possible algorithmic extension, while noting verification difficulty.
- Theorem 2.14: $2$-part twist theorem; no semistability hypothesis in its statement.
- Remark 2.15: full rational $2$-torsion missing.
- Remark 2.19: every curve reaching final BSD$(E,2)$ check has odd analytic Sha; positive $2$-valuation would require higher descent, but does not occur in that frontier.

URL:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16044

## Fouquet–Wan

Olivier Fouquet, Xin Wan,
*The Iwasawa Main Conjecture for universal families of modular motives*,
arXiv:2107.13726v3.

Key results:

- Theorem 1.1: convenient sufficient residual hypotheses.
- Theorem 1.7: arbitrary reduction type at $p$.
- Corollary 1.10: $p$-part of BSD for a GL2-type abelian variety / elliptic curve when central $L$-value is nonzero and the residual hypotheses hold.

URL:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13726

## Burungale–Castella–Skinner

Ashay Burungale, Francesc Castella, Christopher Skinner,
*Base change and Iwasawa Main Conjectures for GL2*,
arXiv:2405.00270v2.

Gives non-semistable / ramification-relaxed good-ordinary support under residual hypotheses.

URL:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00270

## Yan–Zhu

Xiaojun Yan, Xiuwu Zhu,
*Main conjectures for non-CM elliptic curves at good ordinary primes*,
arXiv:2412.20078v4.

Rank $\le1$ p-converse / p-part BSD under good-ordinary residual hypotheses.

URL:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20078

## Full rational 2-torsion reconnaissance

Zhangjie Wang, Shenxing Zhang,
*On the quadratic twist of elliptic curves with full 2-torsion*,
arXiv:2303.05058.

Shows substantial 2-Selmer / $2$-primary Sha structure for special full-$2$-torsion families, but is not used here as a replacement full-BSD family theorem.

URL:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05058
