# Primary Sources

## Fouquet–Wan

Olivier Fouquet, Xin Wan,
*The Iwasawa Main Conjecture for universal families of modular motives*,
arXiv:2107.13726v3.

Important distinction:

### Theorem 1.1
The simple sufficient interface requires:
1. global absolute irreducibility;
2. local semisimplification equal neither to
   \(\chi\oplus\chi\) nor to
   \(\chi\oplus\chi_{\rm cyc}\chi\);
3. an auxiliary \(\ell\parallel N\) with the stated inertia/fixed-space
   conditions.

### Theorem 1.7
The exact main theorem requires:
1. global absolute irreducibility;
2. local semisimplification not of
   \(\chi\oplus\chi_{\rm cyc}\chi\);
3. a specified ramified local extension at an auxiliary \(\ell\), with the
   nontrivial unramified quadratic character in the relevant case.

For the witness-network route with a nonsplit multiplicative auxiliary
prime, `FW17_EXACT` is the preferred theorem profile.

Corollary 1.10 gives the p-part BSD formula when \(L(A,1)\ne0\), with the
modular-form period.

## Pannekoek

René Pannekoek,
*On p-torsion of p-adic elliptic curves with additive reduction*,
arXiv:1211.5833.

Theorem 1 determines when the good-reduction subgroup
\(E_0(\mathbf Q_p)\) can carry nontrivial p-torsion, with exceptional small
primes \(2,3,5,7\).  This is useful as a cheap torsion prefilter, but it does
not rule out a nontrivial quadratic kernel character and therefore is not a
complete FW-H2 test.

## Keller–Yin

Timo Keller, Mulun Yin,
*p-converse theorems for elliptic curves of potentially good ordinary
reduction at Eisenstein primes*,
arXiv:2410.23241.

This confirms that potentially good ordinary additive reduction and
residually reducible \(E[p]\) form a genuine nonempty arithmetic regime.
It is therefore unsafe to infer FW-H2 from potential reduction type alone.
