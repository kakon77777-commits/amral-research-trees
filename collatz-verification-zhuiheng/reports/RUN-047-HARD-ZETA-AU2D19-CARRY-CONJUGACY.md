## The change of variable

A-U.2d.18 ended with the mechanical affine cocycle

> `U_{ℓ+1} = (2^{a_{ℓ+1}}U_ℓ − 2^{−m_{ℓ+1}})/3`

and pointed at the multiplier `2^{a}/3` as the place to look for spectral contraction. This round's first move is to show there is nothing there. Set

> `W_ℓ := 2^{−ε_ℓ}U_ℓ`,  and since `2^{m_ℓ+ε_ℓ} = 2^{Q_ℓ}/3^ℓ`,  `W_ℓ = 3^ℓV_ℓ/2^{Q_ℓ}`

and the multiplier vanishes:

> `W_{ℓ+1} = W_ℓ − 3^ℓ/2^{Q_{ℓ+1}}`.

One line of algebra — `3V_{ℓ+1} = 2^{q}V_ℓ − 1` and `Q_{ℓ+1} = Q_ℓ + q` — and the whole real process is a strictly decreasing one-dimensional carry. Every decrement is positive, so `W_0 = Z > W_1 > … > W_h = 2^{−ε_h}X`, and since `X > Z` the carry never leaves `(Z/2, Z]`.

That is a genuinely useful reduction and it is entirely checkable: `W_ℓ` is an exact rational, the recurrence is exact, the band is exact, and the iterated form `W_ℓ = Z − Σ_{j≤ℓ}3^{j−1}/2^{Q_j}` is exact. All four hold on every one of the 1264 bridges and 4433 steps.

The consequence the round draws from it is negative and correct: **NO-GO 11.1**, there is no hidden Archimedean spectral contraction, because Theorem 5.1 makes the mechanical multiplier an exact coboundary — `∏_{j=r+1}^{s}2^{a_j}/3 = 2^{ε_s−ε_r} ∈ (1/2,2)` — with no exponential growth or decay to find on any interval.

## Finding 1 — the frontier and the checker report disagree on β

and `Hard_Zeta_AU2d19_checker_report.json` publishes

> `"beta": 1.584962500721156`,  `"beta_minus_1": 0.5849625007211561`.

The report's values are machine-generated by the shipped script, which computes `BETA = log2(3)` and `BETA - 1`. The frontier's are hand-written, and they are wrong: `1.5849625007211563` is **one ulp above** the nearest double to `log₂3`, and `0.5849625007211563` is one ulp above the nearest double to `β−1` and **two ulps** from what `log2(3) - 1.0` actually returns. So the frontier's two numbers match neither the correctly rounded value nor the float64 route their own script takes.

Nothing downstream depends on them — no theorem in the round has a numeric constant in it, and the checker never reads the frontier — so the consequence is provenance, not arithmetic. But this is the first round of the sweep where two artifacts in the same bundle publish different values for the same named quantity, and it is worth saying which one to trust: the report.

This is recorded as a finding rather than counted as a gate failure. The gate's `disagreeing_with_both_evaluations` counter still guards the *report's* constants — the ones a generator should have got right — and those are clean.

## Finding 2 — the sharper window's strict inequality is attained

> `2^{m_ℓ+ε_ℓ−ε_h}X ≤ V_ℓ < 2^{m_ℓ+ε_ℓ}Z`

The lower end is attained at `ℓ = h`, where `V_h = X` and the exponent collapses — the `≤` is correct and deliberate. The upper end is attained at `ℓ = 0`, where `m_0 = 0`, `ε_0 = 0`, `W_0 = Z` and `V_0 = Z` exactly, so `V_0 < 2^0·Z` is **false**. Both ends are attained exactly once per bridge, on all 1264.

Corollary 4.2's phase-free consequence `2^{m_ℓ−1}Z < V_ℓ < 2^{m_ℓ+1}Z` is strict at both ends and holds everywhere, and it is what the shipped checker tests — so the strictness in the intermediate boxed line is never exercised. The mathematics is unaffected; the sign is.

## Finding 3 — the third synthetic block, and it also cannot fail

`mesoscopic_modulus_algebra` runs 10,000 iterations of

```python
kcrit = ceil(log(target, 3))
assert 3**kcrit >= target
if kcrit > 0: assert 3**(kcrit-1) < target
k = max(0, kcrit-2); assert 3**k < target
```

The first two assertions are the definition of a ceiling. The third is implied by the second, since `3^{k−2} < 3^{k−1}`. All three are computed in float64, so the only way any could fail is a rounding that puts `kcrit` on the wrong side of an integer — and over the same sampling the closest `log₃(target)` comes to an integer is `4.6 × 10⁻⁵`, against a double's error near `10⁻¹⁵`: a margin of **46 billion to one**, with zero samples that could have failed.

That is the third round in a row with a large synthetic block that carries no information: RUN-046 found two in A-U.2d.18 (`near_linear_gap_algebra` and `positive_lift_drop_algebra`), and this is the third. In each case the block is honestly scoped and the count is honestly reported — the issue is only what a reader takes `10000` to mean.

The other two synthetic blocks this round are **not** of that kind. `mechanical_neutrality` genuinely tests that `2^{⌈βs⌉−⌈βr⌉}/3^{s−r}` stays inside `(1/2,2)`, which a wrong ceiling would break. `valuation_aliasing` genuinely tests Theorem 8.1.
