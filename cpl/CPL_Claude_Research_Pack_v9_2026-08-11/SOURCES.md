# v9 Primary Sources

## Claude 2026 paper
*More Than Two Thirds of the Zeros of the Riemann Zeta Function Lie on the Critical Line*.

Key places:
- Proposition 5.6: prime polynomial quadratic mean, diagonal plus off-diagonal.
- §7.5: $\lambda\le1$ limitation; beyond it requires Hardy–Littlewood-strength prime-pair information.
- Remark 1.1: $70/80/90\%$ support targets.

https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/564f962e60643842f5fcb4a17c9dbc8f608f1c37.pdf

## D. A. Goldston
*Notes on Pair Correlation of Zeros and Prime Numbers*, arXiv:math/0412313.

Key places:
- §6: strong Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair conjecture with $O(N^{1/2+\varepsilon})$ error.
- It implies SPC for $1\le\alpha\le2-\varepsilon$.
- §9: equivalence with prime variance in short intervals; the average second-moment hypothesis gives the full SPC range.

https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412313

## Montgomery–Soundararajan
*Primes in Short Intervals*, arXiv:math/0409258 / CMP 252 (2004).

Relevant for the conjectural second-moment / Gaussian short-interval picture.

https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0409258

## Goldston–Lee–Schettler–Suriajaya
*Pair Correlation Conjecture for the zeros of the Riemann zeta-function I: simple and critical zeros*, arXiv:2503.15449.

Full PCC implies asymptotically 100% simple and critical zeros without assuming RH.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15449
