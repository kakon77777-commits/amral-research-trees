# CPL v11 Sources

## Claude 2026 paper
*More Than Two Thirds of the Zeros of the Riemann Zeta Function Lie on the Critical Line.*

Relevant:
- Proposition 5.6: exact $O_1$ formula and $|O_1|\ll L^2X$.
- Theorem 5.8 and Remark 5.10: prime-side trace and relation to unconditional Montgomery.
- §7.5: the $\sigma=1$ barrier and need for Hardy–Littlewood / $\alpha>1$ pair correlation.

https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/564f962e60643842f5fcb4a17c9dbc8f608f1c37.pdf

## Goldston 2004
D. A. Goldston, *Notes on Pair Correlation of Zeros and Prime Numbers.*

Relevant:
- (6.3): strong Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair hypothesis.
- §9: pair correlation and short-interval prime variance; the second-moment hypothesis is weaker than pointwise HL.

https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412313

## Zaccagnini 2016
A. Zaccagnini, *The Selberg integral and a new pair-correlation function for the zeros of the Riemann zeta-function.*

Relevant:
- definition of $J(x,\theta)$;
- recorded unconditional range for $J=o(x^3\theta^2)$.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.02952

## BGSTB unconditional Montgomery theorem
S. A. C. Baluyot, D. A. Goldston, A. I. Suriajaya, C. L. Turnage-Butterbaugh,
*An unconditional Montgomery Theorem for Pair Correlation of Zeros of the Riemann Zeta Function.*

Relevant:
- unconditional form factor is real, even, and nonnegative;
- Montgomery asymptotic on $0\le\alpha\le1$.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04799

## CGdL 2020
A. Chirre, F. Gonçalves, D. de Laat,
*Pair correlation estimates for the zeros of the zeta function via semidefinite programming.*

Relevant:
- $\widehat f\le0$ outside $[-1,1]$;
- tail-sign use of $F\ge0$;
- under RH, multiplicity constant $1.3208$ and simple-zero proportion $67.92\%$;
- equation (10), the scalar zero-side lower bound whose published proof uses RH.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08843

## Short-interval higher uniformity
K. Matomäki, M. Radziwiłł, X. Shao, T. Tao, J. Teräväinen,
*Higher uniformity of arithmetic functions in short intervals II. Almost all intervals.*

Relevant:
- for $\Lambda$, almost-all-interval uniformity at $H\ge X^{1/3+\varepsilon}$;
- short-averaged Hardy–Littlewood consequences, but not the exact WPPH second-trace statistic.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05770
